General danger signs > CHECK FOR GENERAL DANGER SIGNS
TC-02 Cough / Respiratory EN: 14-month-old child with cough, breathing rate 48 breaths/min, visible lower chest wall indrawing (subcostal indrawing).
AR: طفل عمره 14 شهراً يعاني من كحة وتنفس سريع (48 نفس/د) مع انسحاب أسفل جدار الصدر للداخل عند الشهيق. 		🔴 RED (Urgent Referral) Classify as SEVERE PNEUMONIA OR VERY SEVERE DISEASE. Give first dose of appropriate antibiotic. Refer URGENTLY to hospital for oxygen and IV antibiotics. Page 20–23
Cough or difficult breathing > SEVERE PNEUMONIA
TC-03 Cough / Respiratory EN: 9-month-old infant with cough for 4 days, breathing rate 54 breaths/min. No danger signs, no chest indrawing, no stridor.
AR: رضيع عمره 9 أشهر يعاني من كحة منذ 4 أيام، معدل التنفس 54 نفس/د. لا توجد علامات خطورة ولا انسحاب للصدر. 		🟡 YELLOW (Clinic Treatment) Classify as PNEUMONIA (Fast breathing: >=50 for 2-12m). Give oral Cotrimoxazole or Amoxicillin for 5 days. Soothe throat with safe remedy. Follow-up in 2 days. Page 20, 22–23
Cough or difficult breathing > PNEUMONIA
TC-04 Cough / Respiratory EN: 8-month-old infant with cough and runny nose for 3 days. Breathing rate is 36 breaths/min. No chest indrawing.
AR: رضيع عمره 8 أشهر يعاني من كحة ورشح، معدل التنفس 36 نفس/د. لا توجد صعوبة تنفس أو سحب للصدر. 		🟢 GREEN (Home Care) Classify as NO PNEUMONIA: COUGH OR COLD. No antibiotics needed. Advise mother to continue feeding/breastfeeding, soothe throat with safe remedy. Follow-up in 5 days if not improving. Page 20, 22, 23
Cough or difficult breathing > NO PNEUMONIA
TC-05 Diarrhoea / Dehydration EN: Child with watery diarrhoea for 3 days, lethargic/unconscious, eyes sunken, skin pinch goes back very slowly (>2 seconds).
AR: طفل مصاب بإسهال مائي منذ 3 أيام، فاقد للوعي/خامل، العيون غائرة، وثنية جلد البطن ترجع ببطء شديد. 		🔴 RED (Severe / Plan C) Classify as SEVERE DEHYDRATION. Start IV fluids immediately (Plan C: Ringer's Lactate 100 ml/kg) or urgent hospital referral with ORS sips. Page 28, 143
Diarrhoea > SEVERE DEHYDRATION (Plan C)
TC-06 Diarrhoea / Dehydration EN: Child with diarrhoea, restless and irritable, sunken eyes, drinking eagerly and thirsty, skin pinch goes back slowly.
AR: طفل مصاب بإسهال، متململ وسريع الانفعال، عيونه غائرة، يقبل على الشرب بلهفة وعطش، ثنية الجلد ترجع ببطء. 		🟡 YELLOW (Clinic Treatment) Classify as SOME DEHYDRATION. Treat with ORS solution in clinic over 4 hours (Plan B: 75 ml/kg). Reassess after 4 hours. Continue breastfeeding. Page 28–29, 98
Diarrhoea > SOME DEHYDRATION (Plan B)
TC-07 Diarrhoea / Dysentery EN: 2-year-old child with loose stools containing visible fresh blood and mucus for 2 days.
AR: طفل عمره سنتين يعاني من إسهال مع وجود دم صريح ومخاط في البراز لمدة يومين. 		🟡 YELLOW (Clinic Treatment) Classify as DYSENTERY. Treat for 5 days with oral antibiotic recommended for Shigella (e.g. Ciprofloxacin/Cotrimoxazole). Prevent dehydration. Follow-up in 2 days. Page 26, 30
Diarrhoea > DYSENTERY
TC-08 Fever / Meningitis EN: 3-year-old child with high fever (39°C) and stiff neck (resistance when bending head forward toward chest).
AR: طفل عمره 3 سنوات يعاني من حمى شديدة (39°C) مع تيبس وتصلب في الرقبة عند محاولة ثني الرأس للأمام. 		🔴 RED (Urgent Referral) Classify as VERY SEVERE FEBRILE DISEASE (Suspect Meningitis). Give first dose of IM/IV antibiotic (Chloramphenicol/Ampicillin). Treat to prevent hypoglycemia. Refer URGENTLY to hospital. Page 35, 37–38
Fever > VERY SEVERE FEBRILE DISEASE
TC-09 Fever / Measles EN: Child with generalized measles rash, red eyes, clouding of the cornea (hazy cornea) and deep extensive mouth ulcers.
AR: طفل مصاب بطفح الحصبة، احمرار بالعين، عتامة في القرنية، وتقرحات فموية عميقة وممتدة تمنعه من الشرب. 		🔴 RED (Urgent Referral) Classify as SEVERE COMPLICATED MEASLES. Give high-dose Vitamin A immediately. Apply Tetracycline eye ointment. Give first dose of antibiotic. Refer URGENTLY. Page 36, 41
Fever > SEVERE COMPLICATED MEASLES
TC-10 Ear Problem EN: Child with ear pain, ear discharge for 3 days, and tender swelling behind the ear over the mastoid bone.
AR: طفل يعاني من ألم وإفرازات بالأذن منذ 3 أيام مع وجود تورم مؤلم خلف الأذن فوق عظمة الخشاء. 		🔴 RED (Urgent Referral) Classify as MASTOIDITIS. Give first dose of intramuscular antibiotic and first dose of Paracetamol for pain. Refer URGENTLY to hospital. Page 44–45
Ear problem > MASTOIDITIS
TC-11 Malnutrition & Anaemia EN: 18-month-old child with visible severe wasting (skin and bones / marasmus) and pitting oedema of both feet.
AR: طفل عمره 18 شهراً يعاني من هزال شديد واضح (جلد على عظم) مع تورم وانطباع في القدمين عند الضغط (وذمة). 		🔴 RED (Urgent Referral) Classify as SEVERE MALNUTRITION OR SEVERE ANAEMIA (Kwashiorkor/Marasmus). Give single dose Vitamin A. Refer URGENTLY to hospital for specialized nutritional support. Page 48–50
Malnutrition and anaemia > SEVERE MALNUTRITION
TC-12 Young Infant (<2 Months) EN: 3-week-old young infant with breathing rate 66 breaths/min, axillary temperature 35.2°C (hypothermia), and expiratory grunting.
AR: رضيع عمره 3 أسابيع، معدل التنفس 66 نفس/د، حرارته 35.2°C (انخفاض حرارة) مع وجود شخير عند الزفير (Grunting). 		🔴 RED (Urgent Referral) Classify as POSSIBLE SERIOUS BACTERIAL INFECTION. Give first dose of IM Ampicillin/Benzylpenicillin + Gentamicin. Prevent hypoglycemia. Keep infant warm during transit. Refer URGENTLY. Page 62–64, 86
Sick Young Infant (1 week–2m) > POSSIBLE SERIOUS BACTERIAL INFECTION
TC-13 Young Infant (<2 Months) EN: What are the four signs of good attachment when a mother breastfeeds her young infant?
AR: ما هي العلامات الأربع الصحيحة لالتصاق الرضيع بثدي الأم أثناء الرضاعة الطبيعية؟ 		🟢 GREEN (Counseling) Teach mother the 4 signs: 1. Chin touching breast, 2. Mouth wide open, 3. Lower lip turned outward, 4. More areola visible above than below mouth. Page 68, 117
Sick Young Infant > Assess breastfeeding (Attachment)
